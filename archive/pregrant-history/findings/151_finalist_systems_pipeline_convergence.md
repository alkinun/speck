read-only checkpoint paths and one dense/candidate GPU load/compiled step; qualify live memory/disk/
driver/package/PID sampler behavior; produce real compiled-graph/fallback/OOM/CUDA-phase evidence;

implies execution or systems claims. Further live systems work during the language sequence is forbidden.
