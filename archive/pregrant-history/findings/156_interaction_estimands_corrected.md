# 156 — Pairwise cube interactions now have exact estimands and multiplicity

## V1 ambiguity

For each axis pair, V1 listed two conditional difference-of-differences but named one pairwise term in
the four-member Holm family. It did not say whether SD/SW/DW meant one conditional, both separately, or
their factorial average.

## V2 correction

Each primary pairwise term is now the arithmetic mean of its two conditional contrasts. SD, SW, DW,
and SDW form the exact Holm claim family. All six conditionals are still published and must each clear
the harmful language/source margin as intersection safety guards; they receive no uncorrected positive
claim.

Sixteen complete coefficient vectors cover three main effects, three averaged pairwise terms, SDW, six
conditionals, and three full-model removals. The validator reconstructs every coefficient rather than
checking names alone.

Synthetic proofs recover 2/3/4 main effects from an additive cube, a pure SD=5 interaction at both W
levels, and the critical hidden-harm case: +0.02 at W=0 and −0.02 at W=1 average to zero but fail the
+0.01 conditional guard.

The design changes no resources: eight cells × three paired seeds remains 24 runs. Axes are unselected,
the cube is unmaterialized, v2 is unregistered, and training/promotion remain blocked.
