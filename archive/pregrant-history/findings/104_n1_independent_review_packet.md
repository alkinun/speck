# 104 — Frozen N1 independent-review packet

## Purpose

N1's internal audit now favors retirement, but the required review cannot be called independent if
Speck performs it itself. A frozen packet therefore pins the exact residual claim, latest landscape,
claim table, artifact inventory, and seven mandatory paper notes before any future N1 outcome exists.

## Review contract

At least two independent reviewers with hybrid-architecture and experimental-design coverage must write
separate reviews before reconciliation. They must decide whether the residual teaches a non-procedural
causal law or merely replaces mature teacher-guided conversion with costlier from-scratch search. Each
review must name the strongest overlap, unique prediction, fatal falsifier, minimum evidence, estimated
cost, and conflicts.

Only unanimous support can produce a preregistration *draft*, still without execution. Disagreement
keeps N1 landscape-only and requires a third review. Otherwise the evidence-favored disposition is to
retire N1 and search for another architecture contribution.

## Decision

No independent review is complete. N1 is not formally retired, but no preregistration, experiment,
artifact download/execution, architecture freeze, or paper-scale training is authorized.
