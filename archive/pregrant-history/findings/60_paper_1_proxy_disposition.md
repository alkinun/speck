# 60 — Paper 1 proxy disposition frozen before candidates

## Question

What may the six-cell proxy result authorize, and how do we prevent its observed quality, source,
runtime, or censoring pattern from changing the next experiment after the fact?

## Frozen branches

Before candidate pair 0 and after the control-only target lock, four mutually exclusive dispositions
are fixed:

1. Missing/incomplete/integrity-failed evidence produces no proxy decision and stops progression.
2. A failed aggregate fixed-token non-inferiority bound fails the whole-architecture proxy.
3. Aggregate non-inferiority with any failed source guardrail also fails the proxy; averaging cannot
   hide the source failure.
4. Aggregate non-inferiority plus every source guardrail passes the whole-architecture quality screen
   and authorizes materialization—not execution—of the already designed twelve-run finalist stage.

Time-to-quality censoring remains explicit and cannot create a complete-case estimate. Fixed-FLOP and
fixed-steady-time results are descriptive and cannot rescue failed quality. No branch changes the
fixed three-pair sample or adds post-result runs.

## Authority boundary

Even a pass cannot attribute the result to KDA, NoPE, GQA, or the 3:1 mixer ratio and cannot promote an
architecture. It only makes the six-pair, three-seed-by-two-data-order finalist eligible for separate
materialization, storage, hardware, and analysis qualification at 1,539,833,856 tokens per run.
