# 183 — Science paper and PDF rights require human decision

The review packet preserves exact-revision cards for peS2o, FinePDFs-Edu, Common Pile arXiv and
PubMed, and Proof-Pile-2, together with ODC-By and Common Crawl terms. It is an operational inventory,
not legal advice or acceptance.

The most important gap is source-specific. The pinned peS2o repository contains v3 shards labeled
`v3-fos-license`, but its pinned card still calls v2 “latest” and does not document v3 construction or
license-selection semantics. FinePDFs exposes PDF URLs and extraction metadata but no per-document
content license. Common Pile preserves arXiv/PubMed license metadata but explicitly warns that
license laundering and inaccurate metadata can misassign rights. Proof-Pile arXiv exposes paper IDs
and URLs but no per-paper license and declares no aggregate license.

A named human must approve or reject each source, the exact accepted license set, attribution and
share-alike delivery, Common Crawl/underlying-PDF risk, corpus redistribution, and removal/rebuild
controls. peS2o v3 needs authoritative documentation or written confirmation; Proof-Pile needs a
reliable per-paper license mapping or rejection. Until an auditable acceptance record exists, every
science successor remains blocked.
