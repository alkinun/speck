Exact-revision cards for FineMath, MegaMath, OpenWebMath, and Proof-Pile-2 are preserved with hashes,
alongside ODC-By 1.0 and the Common Crawl terms retrieved on 2026-09-07. This is an operational review
packet, not legal advice or automated acceptance.

FineMath, InfiWebMath, MegaMath Web-Pro, and OpenWebMath are substantially web-derived. ODC-By covers
database rights rather than every underlying page. OpenWebMath explicitly says it does not alter
underlying licenses. Common Crawl's current terms make use a binding acceptance event and expressly

The code-derived sources have a separate chain. Proof-Pile-2 declares no aggregate license and says
underlying licenses remain unchanged. MegaMath Code supplies The Stack v2 metadata and retrieves
Software Heritage blobs; blob availability and cryptographic identity are not permission. The
bounded adapters conservatively admit only records carrying allowlisted permissive-license metadata,
but a human must approve that allowlist, metadata reliability, notices, and intended scope.

The packet proposes immutable URL/repository/blob lineage, dataset credits, per-file notice bundles,
a named removal contact, deny ledgers, rebuildable packed shards, and a separate redistribution
decision. A named authority must record approved source IDs, exact evidence hashes, organizational
scope, Common Crawl acceptance, attribution placement, original-code license policy, redistribution,
removal ownership, date, and auditable approval. Until then, all six math successors remain blocked.
