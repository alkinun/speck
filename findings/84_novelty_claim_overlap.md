# 84 — Claim-granular novelty overlap and priority

## Outcome

The twelve-source landscape is now resolved at claim granularity rather than by paper title. Fourteen
candidate statements were mapped to their strongest source and an explicit decision.

Joint hybrid placement, qualitative local/global roles, layer importance, adaptive head/layer/sequence
budgets, requested-cost attention, recurrent prefix checkpoints, reasoning-aware heads, routing versus
storage, all-hop source conjunction, missing-source recovery, long-context budgeted evidence
compression, and structured candidate evidence verification are direct or adjacent prior art. They are
mandatory baselines or non-claims, not ingredients that become novel when bundled.

## Research priority

N1—the prospective from-scratch placement law—is the only remaining primary architecture hypothesis.
It must fit discovery layouts and predict unseen layouts, tasks, scales, and a different recurrent/cache
representation without optimizing on those held-out cells. Count, FLOPs, final-layer presence, uniform/
random/role layouts, sensitivity, greedy replacement, FlashMorph-style gates, ASA roles, and
SqueezeAttention clustering are mandatory baselines. A fixed-count/state/FLOP intervention must move
both the predicted role diagnostic and held-out quality.

N2's conjunction concept is rejected. Its residual question is only whether a cheap pre-output internal-
state statistic provides incremental empirical prediction beyond external proposition coverage and LLM
sufficiency judgments plus all internal routing/cache metrics. That work is deferred before a protocol
or compute allocation until claim-granular independent expert review finds material value. Even a
positive result may be an evaluation contribution rather than architecture novelty.

## Decision discipline

Unavailable upstream code remains a reproducibility problem and never restores novelty. No experiment
will be launched solely to rescue an overlapped claim. N1 remains unestablished; N2 has no experiment
authority; no new mechanism, novelty gate, architecture freeze, training, or paper-scale authority is
created.
