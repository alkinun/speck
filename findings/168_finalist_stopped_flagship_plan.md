# 168 — Finalist chain stopped; program replaced by the flagship plan

On 2026-09-06 the six-pair finalist chain was stopped deliberately during dense control pair 0,
attempt 2, about 7.5 hours after its 03:40 launch. The path trigger was stopped first, then the
training service. No checkpoint, result, transition, or target lock was written; the data volume
holds only the six completed proxy runs. The HELMET path units were stopped at the same time.

The reason is a change of plan, not a failure. A 5,000 GH200-hour EuroHPC allocation on one
four-GPU node makes every remaining question in this program a one-hour to one-day job there, and
the finalist's twelve runs would cost about sixty GPU-hours on the node against five days here.
The three-pair proxy in finding 105 remains the 150M replication evidence.

The novelty gate, the tri-axis title, the HELMET and NoLiMa integrations, and the readiness
contracts for HCA, CSA, AttnRes, Stable LatentMoE, interactions, and scaling are retired from
Paper 1 and kept as archived future work. The operating plan is now
[`research/flagship/PLAN.md`](../research/flagship/PLAN.md).
