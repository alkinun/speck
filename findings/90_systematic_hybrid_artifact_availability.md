# 90 — Systematic hybrid-study artifact availability

## Audit

The arXiv v3 record and full text provide a CC-BY-4.0 paper, mathematical descriptions, aggregate
training settings, architecture tables, and result tables. They do not declare an official repository,
model collection, exact configuration bundle, evaluation implementation, or immutable data-order
manifest for the systematic Transformer/Mamba experiments.

This makes the paper a strong conceptual and quantitative baseline but does not make its behavior
reproducible from an identified artifact. Paper reuse rights are not code, model, or data identity.

## Decision

No upstream execution or reproduction is authorized. Across sixteen audited sources, repository and
root-rights counts remain unchanged, and no new full reproduction path is qualified. Because N1 itself
has no experiment authority, seeking author artifacts is conditional on independent review first
finding that prospective unseen-layout prediction retains enough incremental scientific value.
