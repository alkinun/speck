# 88 — Novelty priority correction after N1 audit

## Correction

The v1 claim table ranked N1 as the only primary architecture hypothesis. The subsequent role, scaling,
ratio/mixer, greedy-placement, and artifact audits make that label too strong. N1's broad role framing is
overlapped, and its residual prospective prediction question is not itself a model mechanism.

The v2 table therefore records zero established architecture-novelty candidates. N1 survives only as a
possible empirical architecture law: predict unseen non-uniform placements trained from scratch without
observing their outcomes, beyond all role/scaling/probe/greedy/joint-gate baselines. It receives
literature, artifact, patent, deployed-system, and independent expert review only; no placement
experiment is authorized.

N2 remains a deferred empirical diagnostic question after its source-conjunction concept was rejected.
It also has no protocol or experiment authority.

## Baseline independence

The final Paper 1 candidate continues because its three-pair comparison was frozen earlier and estimates
whether the conservative five-cache KDA/GQA parent improves quality/cost versus dense attention. This is
valuable architecture evidence even if neither N1 nor N2 is novel. A successful proxy may retain the
parent for future isolated research, but cannot satisfy the separate novelty or paper-scale gates.

## Decision

There is no current Speck architecture freeze. No new training begins from either residual law. After the
proxy result, novelty research remains evidence-first: complete the landscape and independent review,
then either formulate a genuinely distinct falsifiable mechanism/law or explicitly position the work as
replication/systems evidence.
