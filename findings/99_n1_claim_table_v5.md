# 99 — N1 claim table after HALO

## Claim correction

HALO's hidden-state-aligned, recall/commonsense-guided single-replacement selection is now a mandatory
conversion baseline. HypeNet's positional encoding, recurrent QK normalization, GQA-to-MHA expansion,
output gates, and Lightning Attention are a direct composition-overlap row and cannot be claimed as a
Speck bundle.

The only formal N1 difference is held-out prediction of jointly trained from-scratch layouts, including
their interactions. That is a procedural residual, not an architecture mechanism. No evidence shows it
is feasible, transferable, causal, or more useful than HALO, DtR, or FlashMorph conversion selection.

## Decision

There are still zero established architecture-novelty candidates. N1 requires independent retention-or-
retirement review and has no experiment authority. N2 remains deferred. The frozen baseline continues
independently and cannot establish either claim.
