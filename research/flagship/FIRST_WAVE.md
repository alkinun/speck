# First wave after the long-context pivot

The former 29-slot / 192-GPU-hour E1W/E1S/E3 proposal is retired. Its JSON and prepared source records
remain reproducibility inputs; they do not authorize new allocation runs or full-wave acquisition.
[The transition](PIVOT.md) records scope and preserved state.

## Before access

- Reconcile finite preparation workers and reuse verified source/token caches.
- Build a measured successor source-capacity/exposure sheet for the broad base and study inputs.
- Retain coherent-unit identities and prepare disjoint document/history task pilots.
- Freeze candidate evaluation versions and source-family exclusions before task generation.
- Prepare exact model shapes, storage/accounting, recovery commands and R0 bounded inputs.
- Audit novelty against Olmo Hybrid, ProLong, HELMET, LongPO and contemporary task-training work.

## R0: 70 GPU-hours

Qualify GH200 arm64 dependencies, FLA KDA, four-GPU DDP, optimizer, exact-shape 1.2B 4K/32K/128K fit,
forward/backward/resume and checkpoint/storage overhead. Record numerical and cached-generation parity.
Measure end-to-end throughput, not a theoretical TFLOP/s constant. A failed 128K path activates length
fallback; it does not require an unbudgeted context-parallel rewrite.

## R1: 80 GPU-hours

Qualify the broad recipe's supply/repetition and bounded LR region for both architectures. Use
independent task pilots to detect floor/ceiling and scoring failures. Materialize the entire R2/R3 run
list and cost forecast, including parent/common continuation/branches. Freeze numerical quality floors,
non-inferiority margins, datasets, scoring, endpoints and transfer route before R2 confirmation outputs.

R2 is three paired seed blocks (six parents, twelve supervision branches) within 360 hours. R3 has
one preselected scale/horizon route within 180; R4 supplies 60 hours of scientific evaluation/analysis.
See [STUDY.md](STUDY.md). No old first-wave launch manifest is a substitute for this freeze.
