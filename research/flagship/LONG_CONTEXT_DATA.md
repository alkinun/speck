## Broad base

Use the starting web/code/math/synthetic/science/reference prior **55/15/10/10/5/5** with FineWeb-Edu,
restricted Stack v3, FineMath-4+, Cosmopedia v2, peS2o v3 and FineWiki, subject to actual admitted supply.
This is a broad pre-results default, not an optimized recipe. Freeze the same mixture through the
20% WSD decay by default. R1 qualifies a bounded repetition/optimization schedule, not a large mixture
funnel. Source substitutions need an explicit pre-results supply successor and matching study controls.

The old conditional 150B-unique operating envelope is a planning boundary; it is not a completed corpus.
Count source/family exposures through base, decay, continuation and SFT. Average 320B/150B reuse cannot
justify excessive repetition in scarce categories. Preserve source-separated shards so weights do not
require tokenization again. Freeze actual quantities, storage, delivery and exposure limits before B0.

## Long units and tasks

Prepare coherent documents/books/manuals, ordered conversation histories and identifiable repository
snapshots. Preserve family identity, revision, section/file/turn order, content hashes and window
positions. Target 2-4B unique retained long-unit tokens, including 0.5-1B in coherent 128K-or-longer
units, only after measuring the budget and supply. These targets are not availability claims.

Natural-document continuation teaches exposure to length. The controlled supervision intervention asks
for relations requiring multiple separated pieces, applying an earlier rule, explicit corrections,
conflicting evidence and absent evidence. Retain supporting spans/turns and verify answers. Removing
one necessary piece or changing a fact should produce the declared change in answerability or answer.
Check shortcuts and annotation artifacts with held-out templates and blinded source-stratified review.

By default, replace 30% of assistant-target supervision with the experimental standard/targeted slice,
retaining 70% broad replay in both. Match processed input-plus-target tokens, supervised tokens, source
frames, lengths and answer style within predeclared tolerances. Long prompts count even when their loss
is masked. Teacher generation, rejection and verification costs are reported separately from student
training. Existing external post-training work must have its evidence and compatibility reconciled.

## Packing and separation

The flat loader does not itself establish coherent windows. Use boundary-aligned preprocessing or a
qualified document/window-aware sampler. BOS/EOS is not a recurrent reset or attention mask. Any reset
or independent-example packing policy must qualify both recurrent and global paths.

Separate training, engineering/difficulty pilots, development selection and sealed final tests by
source family (including editions, forks, histories and task variants). Pin benchmark identities and
exclusions before generating supervision. Never recycle benchmark questions/answers into training.
Keep legacy D5/E2 audits unopened and excluded; new tests need new identities, not renamed old audits.
The existing six-category BPB held-out tooling remains a general-retention input.

## Operational migration

- Frozen Mistral bytes and completed counting caches remain reusable under identity parity.
- Old 29-slot, 192-hour, 17.7B first-wave requirements are historical and cannot drive new expansion.
- Existing finite acquisition jobs can finish already recorded units. This pivot does not interrupt,
  restart or extend workers. Reconcile live completion before taking their next steps.
- Reuse completed stocks once; overlapping predecessor banks are subsets, not additional capacity.
- Rights and source-use conditions remain source-specific. New histories, tasks or teacher outputs need
  the applicable review; a new paper theme does not expand prior permission.
- Production and training manifests must bind new quotas, partitions, revisions and operating costs.
  The existing launch receipt path needs qualification for this successor's experiment identities.
