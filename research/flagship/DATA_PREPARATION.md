# Data preparation critical path after the pivot

The selected [data plan](data_plan_v3.json) reuses completed preparation without inheriting obsolete
E1/E3 quotas. Detailed predecessor measurements and explanations are preserved in the
[scope snapshot](../history/2026-09-15-allocation-thesis/research/flagship/DATA_PREPARATION.md).

## Existing evidence

The historical 2B calibration projected 454.22 serial hours (18.93 days) for a 150B path after inputs
were available. That projection excluded additional source exhaustion, engineering, verification and
site delivery. New bounded SQLite/source-bank improvements are not evidence of full-production rate.
The conditional 150B envelope is retained; actual successor throughput and source yield remain open.

## Four separate deliverables

1. R0 bounded synthetic/retained hardware inputs, ready before access.
2. R1/R2 study inputs: broad source streams, paired parents, coherent 32K continuation and verified
   standard/targeted supervision with independent held-out families.
3. B0 production source bank and per-source exposure schedule, transferred and reopened at the site.
4. L0 coherent 32K/128K units and instruction data, with measured length yield and verified labels.

Track completion dates independently. Final mixture weights must not prevent early source-separated
preparation, but new bulk quantities require a costed supply manifest. A winner may not be chosen
because its data happens to exist. Missing capacity triggers a documented pre-results successor.

## First actions

Reconcile the last observed FineWeb/code jobs, preserve verified Math/FineWiki/peS2o/FineMath/Cosmopedia
caches, inventory useful long-unit metadata before cleanup, and map existing material into successor
requirements. Measure acquisition, filtering, reference replay, dedup, tokenization, packing, verification
and site transfer separately, with interruption and retry cost retained.

No day-21 data-ready claim exists. F0 waits for actual source capacity, tokenizer/partition identities,
exposure limits, site delivery and a budget/calendar forecast. R0 can use bounded qualified inputs while
production proceeds; base training cannot start merely because the node is available.
