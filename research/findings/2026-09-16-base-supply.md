# Selected base mixture: demand is much larger than the current specialist stocks

The [generated report](../../paper/tables/base-supply-v1/report.json) and
[table](../../paper/tables/base-supply-v1/table.md) bind the selected data/model/tokenizer contracts,
current source-specific text receipts and their document-token manifests. They replace no prior
measurement and do not revive the retired source-selection experiment program.

At the 320B default horizon, the 55/15/10/10/5/5 prior requires 176B web, 48B code, 32B math, 32B
synthetic, 16B science and 16B reference training tokens. If only the completed stocks in this snapshot
were used, their average exposures would be 28.47× FineMath, 21.49× Cosmopedia, 19.51× peS2o and
27.49× FineWiki. These are conditional demand/stock ratios, not validated or recommended repetition
rates. Joint-view exclusions and packing losses can increase them. Research and capability-stage
exposure are additional and are not included in this base-only calculation.

FineWeb remains unmeasured as completed paired stock in this snapshot while its finite exclusion/cache
pipeline runs. Restricted Stack v3 has acquisition-only evidence; no eligible full-exclusion cache is
qualified here. Stack-Edu is a different source and is not silently substituted. The earlier peS2o and
FineMath prefix banks are included in their successor counts, never added. Natural UltraData-Math is
not silently blended into the selected FineMath allocation. Missing completed receipts do not mean
that upstream sources contain no more available data.

Receipt reconciliation checks source/category, text-result ancestry, exclusion-parent identity,
frozen tokenizer, matching token/document counts and prior complete-reopen evidence. The older
peS2o/FineWiki cache receipts lack the later inline `completed_reopen_pass` field; their separate
post-outage verification is explicitly bound by matching source, manifest hash and counts. Absence of
that inline field was initially rejected; it was not converted into an assumed pass. This report does
not rescan every corpus shard or establish new joint training eligibility.

Before F0/B0, prepare a costed source-by-source acquisition, filtering, storage and delivery forecast;
measure actual available retained supply; and qualify source/family exposure across all phases. If the
selected mixture/horizon cannot be supplied affordably, record a coherent pre-results successor.
Neither an arbitrary repetition cap nor more copies of existing token caches solves this gap. The
150B unique-token envelope remains an operating hypothesis, not a measured global union.

The generator rejects overlapping bank entries, same-category source substitutions, changed inputs,
unverified caches, tokenizer/exclusion ancestry drift and count mismatches. Ten focused tests pass;
the report was regenerated exactly from its bound inputs. These are preparation checks, not evidence
of training-data quality or model capability.
