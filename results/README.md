# Current results

Store compact results from current experiments here. Each record identifies its producing revision,
inputs, analysis, measured resources, and external artifact locations. Keep failed attempts and
interruption lineage with the experiment.

Completed pre-grant measurements are preserved in the
[result archive](../archive/pregrant-history/results/README.md). Current conclusions may cite archived
evidence directly; moving a result does not change its meaning or its producing implementation.

Full predictions, datasets, checkpoints, and logs belong in the runtime artifact store. See the
[artifact policy](../research/DATA_MANAGEMENT.md) and [paper claims](../paper/claims.json).
